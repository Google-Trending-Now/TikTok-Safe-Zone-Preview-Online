Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the placement questions editors run into before posting on the For You Page.

What are the exact TikTok safe zone margins for 1080×1920 videos?

For an organic 1080×1920 TikTok video, leave at least 150 px clear at the top, 420 px at the bottom, 60 px on the left, and 150 px on the right. That gives you an 870×1350 px safe area where hooks and burn-in captions stay visible on the For You Page.

Why do TikTok Ads need a larger bottom margin than organic posts?

Paid TikTok placements stack a Sponsored label and a full-width CTA button such as Shop Now or Learn More above the caption block. You need at least 540 px of bottom clearance and 160 px on the right so the ad button does not cover your subtitles or offer callout.

Where should I place burn-in captions so they stay readable on the For You Page?

Place burn-in captions in the middle-lower safe window—roughly 30% to 40% up from the bottom edge—and keep them centered slightly left of the right-hand engagement icons. Avoid placing text in the bottom 420 px (or 540 px for ads) where usernames, hashtags, and sound tickers overlap.

Are my videos or images uploaded to a server when I preview them here?

No. Your files never leave your device. The previewer reads images and videos locally using your browser's built-in object URL API, so unreleased client edits and ad creatives stay completely private.
